How the work gets made.

A² Studios is also a show: tutorials on combining AI with human creativity — making music, making videos, and encoding yourself as an avatar. What the tools do, an honest pros-and-cons breakdown, how the money works, and how to stay honest in your marketing.

01 Music

“AI Made the Beat — I Made It Mine”

The AI + human production workflow: prompt like a sample crate, split the stems, then do the human part — your drums, your arrangement, your ear.

02 Video

“I Shot This Music Video With Zero Cameras”

AI music videos from your own photos: direct motion like a DP, work in short clips, cut on the beat — and tick YouTube’s synthetic-content box.

03 Avatar

Your face, your voice, your mannerisms — as an AI you can direct. Scale yourself to 40+ languages. One rule: only ever clone yourself.

Non-negotiables

The house rules.

  • Never present AI output as a real person, real event, or real artist.
  • The 3-second rule: if a viewer couldn’t tell it’s AI within 3 seconds, it isn’t labeled enough.
  • Know the disclosure line: AI mastering, stem-splitting, and mixing are standard studio workflow. The line is crossed when AI generates the composition, melody, vocals, or structure.
  • Verify before quoting: AI prices and policies move fast — check the current numbers before going on camera.
