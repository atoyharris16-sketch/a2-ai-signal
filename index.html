Non-negotiables

The house rules.

  • Never present AI output as a real person, real event, or real artist.
  • The 3-second rule: if a viewer couldn’t tell it’s AI within 3 seconds, it isn’t labeled enough.
  • Know the disclosure line: AI mastering, stem-splitting, and mixing are standard studio workflow. The line is crossed when AI generates the composition, melody, vocals, or structure.
  • Verify before quoting: AI prices and policies move fast — check the current numbers before going on camera.
